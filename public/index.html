Frequently Asked Questions

How do I download a YouTube transcript?

Simply paste the YouTube video URL into the search box at the top of this page, click Fetch Transcript, choose your preferred language from the selector, and download the transcript as SRT, TXT, JSON, or WebVTT. The whole process takes less than 15 seconds.

Is Get YT Transcripts completely free?

Yes! Get YT Transcripts is 100% free with no hidden costs, no signup requirements, and no limits on usage. You can download as many YouTube transcripts as you want without creating an account or paying anything.

What YouTube transcript formats are available?

You can download in SRT (SubRip — perfect for video editors and media players), WebVTT (used for web video captions), JSON (structured data for developers), and plain text (TXT — for notes, articles, and general use). SRT and WebVTT formats include timestamps.

Can I translate YouTube subtitles to another language?

Yes. When a YouTube video has translation data available, you'll see a language selector that lets you switch to any translated version. Simply choose your desired language from the dropdown and the transcript updates instantly.

Does this work with auto-generated YouTube captions?

Yes, Get YT Transcripts retrieves both manually created captions and YouTube's automatic speech recognition captions. The tool prefers manually uploaded captions when available, but falls back to auto-generated ones if needed.

Do I need to install anything?

No installation required. Get YT Transcripts works entirely in your browser. Just visit this website, paste a YouTube link, and download your transcript. Works on desktop and mobile browsers.

What if a video doesn't have transcripts?

If a YouTube video has no captions or transcripts available (some creators disable them), the tool will show an error message. This is rare — most YouTube videos have at least auto-generated captions available.
